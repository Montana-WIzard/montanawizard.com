About the Show

What we're doing out here, and why.

The Montana Wizard Podcast started with a simple idea: there's something worth saying about life out here that doesn't make it into the postcards. The wide horizons, sure. But also the stranger stuff — the small-town basketball tournaments that draw four-deep crowds in February, the schemes and scammers who pass through, the long Tuesday afternoons that turn into philosophy.

What You'll Hear

Each episode is a conversation. Sometimes it's two people, sometimes it's a guest with a story to tell, sometimes it's just thinking out loud about a corner of Montana that deserves more attention than it gets. Expect:

  • Thoughtful talk about life in Big Sky Country
  • Offbeat humor and the occasional tangent
  • A little wizardry — call it pattern recognition with personality
  • Real conversations, not soundbites

Where to Listen

New episodes drop bi-weekly. You can stream the audio on this site, watch the video versions on YouTube, or subscribe through Apple Podcasts.

Behind the Mic

The Montana Wizard Podcast is produced under the Big Sky and is part of the larger Mountain Wizard family of projects. Got a story you think we should cover? A guest worth interviewing? Spell suggestions? Drop us a line — we read everything.
